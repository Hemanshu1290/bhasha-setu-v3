How Bhasha Setu Works

AI proposes structured JSON; the backend validates and performs the write — nothing changes in the spreadsheet without your confirmation.

1

Speech-to-text

Browser voice recognition or typed input, in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or English.

2

Intent + entity extraction

Text is converted into structured JSON (name, mobile, city, intent, scheme) — never edited directly into Excel.

3

Validation + matching

The backend looks for an existing citizen before proposing any change, or prepares a new-citizen insert.

4

Confirmation + audit

Every insert, update, or scheme application requires your explicit "Confirm" click and is logged to Updates_Log.
